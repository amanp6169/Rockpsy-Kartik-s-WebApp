About

One DJ, every kind of night.

Karthik — known on stage as Rockpsy Karthik — is a full-time DJ who reads a room and matches it: soft and warm for a Haldi morning, high-energy for a wedding night, sharp and clean for a corporate stage, and unfiltered chaos for a college fest.

Every set is built from his own edits and mixes, not just a playlist on shuffle — tracks stitched together to fit the room, the culture, and the crowd in front of him.
